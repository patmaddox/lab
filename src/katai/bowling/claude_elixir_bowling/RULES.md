# Bowling Scoring Rules

## Basic Scoring
- Each frame consists of up to 2 rolls
- Score is total pins knocked down across all frames
- Game consists of 10 frames

## Regular Frames (1-9)
- Score = sum of pins knocked down in that frame
- Maximum 10 pins per frame

## Strikes
- All 10 pins knocked down on first roll
- Score = 10 + next 2 rolls
- Frame ends immediately

## Spares
- All 10 pins knocked down using both rolls in frame
- Score = 10 + next 1 roll

## 10th Frame (Special Rules)
- If strike or spare: get extra roll(s)
- Strike: 2 extra rolls
- Spare: 1 extra roll
- No strike/spare: frame ends normally
- Maximum 3 rolls in 10th frame

## Maximum Score
- Perfect game = 300 points (12 strikes)
