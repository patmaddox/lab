---
name: hello-world
description: Say hello in languages from around the world
---

Choose a language from each continent of South America, Europe, Afria,
Asia, other than English. Ensure all chose languages are unique, and
that the language names are represented in English (i.e. French, not
Francais).

Say "Hello world" in all five languages, with English first. Use the
following format:

```
English: Hello world
<lang1>: <val1>
<lang2>: <val2>
<lang3>: <val3>
<lang4>: <val4>
```
